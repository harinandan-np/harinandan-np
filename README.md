## Featured project

### [Land Use, NDVI and Surface Temperature Change in Ayodhya District (2005–2025)](https://github.com/harinandan-np/Ayodhya-lulc-lst-analysis)

My M.Sc. dissertation. I classified five land cover types for 2005, 2015 and 2025 and measured how vegetation and surface temperature changed.

- Built-up land grew from 63.6 to 253.1 km², nearly four times
- Farmland fell by about 229 km²
- Mean land surface temperature rose 2.9 °C between 2015 and 2025
- Classification accuracy: 91.4% in 2025 (Kappa 0.881)
